About

I am a PhD candidate at the Complexity Science Hub working on the complex dynamics that link climate change, environmental events and human mobility. My doctorate is hosted at the University of Vienna, and I am a guest researcher in IIASA's Migration and Sustainable Development research group.

My work sits at the intersection of complex systems methods and migration research — I'm interested in how environmental shocks propagate through social and economic networks to shape where and how people move, and in building data-driven tools that make these dynamics legible.
